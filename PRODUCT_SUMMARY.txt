🧠 PSYCHOLOGY ENGINE (mt-core.js)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

RISK DETECTION ALGORITHM
  • Scans journal text for 30+ dangerous thought patterns
  • Patterns matched: "need to make it back", "just one more", "FOMO", 
    "overconfidence", "revenge", "impatience", "fatigue", etc.
  • Returns list of detected risks + emotional interpretation

READINESS SCORE CALCULATION (0-100)
  • Baseline: 70 (neutral)
  • Penalties: -25 for high-risk emotions, -15 for medium-risk, -10 for mild
  • Bonuses: +10 for plan adherence language, +8 for patience/calm
  • Adjusted for fatigue, anxiety, previous losses
  • Final score = 0-100 (0=Red, 55-74=Yellow, 75+=Green)

PERMISSION STATE LOGIC
  • GREEN (75+): Calm, patient, plan-focused → Normal trading allowed
  • YELLOW (55-74): Mild risk detected → Reduce size, fewer trades, strict checklist
  • RED (0-54): High risk → No live trading recommended, simulation mode only

DAILY SCORECARD LOGIC (0-10 Points)
  • Pre-Session Checklist: 1 point (completed before trading)
  • Only Planned Setup: 2 points (traded only planned setups)
  • Correct Timeframe: 1 point (30m or 1H only, no lower)
  • Risk Respected: 2 points (within risk limits)
  • Trade Limit: 1 point (max 2 trades respected)
  • Loss Protocol: 1 point (waited after losses)
  • No Revenge/FOMO: 1 point (stayed disciplined)
  • Post-Session Journal: 1 point (completed reflection)
  
  AUTO-FAIL CONDITIONS (Score = 0):
    • Revenge trade executed
    • Traded after daily $500 stop
    • Moved stop loss further away
    • Used lower timeframe entry
    • Broken-plan trade

MOCK CLAUDE AI RESPONSES
  • Generates contextual coaching messages based on detected risks
  • Different protocols for different emotional states
  • IF-THEN rules for impulse management
  • Status-appropriate advice (Green = execute, Yellow = slow down, Red = sit out)
  • Ready to swap for real Anthropic Claude API (just change the fetch call)

DATA PERSISTENCE
  • localStorage saves all entries as JSON
  • Survives browser close/reopen
  • Clear your cache = data gone (that's why Google Sheets backup is important)
  • Can hold 10,000+ entries (typical usage = 2-5MB storage)

💡 USAGE EXAMPLE (Day 1)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BEFORE TRADING (5 minutes)
  1. Open index.html
  2. Click "Start Journal Check-In"
  3. Answer 4 pre-session questions:
     - "How are you feeling?" → "Calm, focused, slept well"
     - "What's on your mind?" → "No losses from yesterday"
     - "What's your emotional state?" → "Patient, ready to wait"
     - "What's your intention?" → "Execute my setup cleanly"
  4. Complete 3-item pre-session checklist
  5. Click "Run AI Readiness Check" (takes ~1 second)
  6. Get assessment: "🟢 GREEN — Score 82/100"
  7. Read today's trading protocol

TRADE SESSION
  • Follow the protocol
  • You know your permission state
  • Trade your plan

AFTER TRADING (8 minutes)
  1. Go back to journaling.html
  2. Enter your results:
     - P&L: "+225" or "-150" (or blank)
     - Trades executed: "2"
     - Check 8 scorecard items
     - Check any auto-fail conditions
     - Answer 3 reflection questions
  3. Click "Calculate Daily Score & Save"
  4. See your score: "7/10 - Mixed execution"
  5. Entry saved automatically

GO TO DASHBOARD
  1. Refresh index.html
  2. Heatmap updated with today's color
  3. Stats recalculated
  4. Your discipline streak counted

REPEAT DAILY
  1. Build a 20-day streak
  2. Export weekly to Google Sheets
  3. Review your heatmap weekly
  4. Identify patterns (your top 3 risks)
  5. Adjust your protocol accordingly
