Core Philosophy

MindTrade OS Methodology

MindTrade is a trading psychology operating system. It is NOT a market prediction system, signal provider, or trading strategy bot.

The core principle: Track the trader, not just the trades.

We measure success by discipline and emotional control, not P&L. A trader who broke the plan and made $500 had a failed day. A trader who followed the plan exactly and lost $200 had a successful day.

Core Values

  1. Discipline over P&L — Follow the system, win the day. Period.
  2. Process over Outcome — You control your actions, not market results.
  3. Emotional Awareness over Prediction — Know your emotional state before trading.
  4. Risk Protection over Trade Frequency — Miss opportunities. Protect capital.
  5. Long-term Consistency over Short-term Profit — Be the same trader every day.

20-Day Trading Discipline Challenge Rules

The challenge consists of 20 trading days. Each day you're scored 0-10 based on discipline. The trader with the highest average score wins, regardless of P&L.

Execution Rules

1. Maximum 2 trades per day — No more than two live trades, ever.
2. One winner and done — If you make one clean win, you can exit the day.
3. Stop after 2 losses — Two losses in a day, you're done trading.
4. Stop immediately after 1 broken-plan trade — Any trade outside your plan = stop for the day.

Risk Rules

5. Risk per trade: $150–$200 — Exact size limit per trade.
6. Daily personal max loss: $500 hard stop — Lose $500, stop trading immediately.
7. Weekly max loss: $800–$1,000 or 3 red days — If you hit 3 losing days, week is over.

Setup & Timeframe Rules

8. Only 30m and 1H execution — Trade only these timeframes.
9. No lower timeframe entries — Do not enter on 15m, 5m, or 1m candles.
10. Confirmation candle first, execution candle second — See confirmation before entry.

Loss Recovery Rules

11. After a loss, wait one full 30m candle close — Emotional break is mandatory.
12. No trading if market is ranging and unclear — Only trade during structure.

Behaviour Rules

13. Maximum 90 minutes active screen time — Limit fatigue and emotional drain.
14. Pre-session checklist must be completed — Always do this. No exceptions.
15. Post-session journal must be completed — Reflect every day.

Scoring Rules

16. Score every day out of 10 — Use the daily scorecard.
17. Winner is highest plan-adherence score, not highest P&L — Never P&L. Discipline only.

Auto-Fail Conditions (Score = 0/10)

If ANY of these occur, the day is automatically 0/10, even if other rules were followed:

🚩 Executed a revenge trade (breaking plan to make back losses)
🚩 Traded after hitting the $500 daily loss limit
🚩 Moved a stop loss further away on a losing trade
🚩 Used a lower timeframe entry (below 30m)
🚩 Executed a completely broken-plan trade

Permission States: Green / Yellow / Red

Before each session, the AI Coach assesses your emotional readiness and assigns a permission state. This determines your trading rules for that day.

🟢 GREEN (Score 75+)

You sound: Calm, patient, structured, plan-focused.

Permission: Normal execution allowed. A+ setups only.

What to do: Execute your plan. Follow your rules. Trust the process. Nothing to prove today.

🟡 YELLOW (Score 55-74)

You sound: Mild emotional risk. Frustration, impatience, FOMO, overconfidence, tiredness.

Permission: Trade smaller, slower, fewer trades. Strict checklist required.

What to do: Reduce size 25%. Max 1-2 trades. Only trade confirmed setups. Expect to miss moves.

🔴 RED (Score 0-54)

You sound: High emotional risk. Revenge trading, desperation, chasing, anger, broken-plan behaviour.

Permission: No live trading recommended.

What to do: Simulation mode only. Reset protocol. Journal. Walk away. Review rules.

Dangerous Thoughts & What They Signal

The AI Coach listens for these phrases and emotional patterns. When detected, it flags the likely risk and adjusts your permission state.

🔴 Revenge Trading Risk

"I need to make it back"
"Make the loss back quickly"
"I'm desperate to recover"

What it means: You're emotionally attached to the loss and seeking to recoup rather than follow the plan. This leads to oversized risk, broken-plan trades, and blowups. STOP trading immediately if you hear this in your head.

🟡 FOMO & Chasing Risk

"The move is leaving without me"
"I'm missing out"
"I missed the move"

What it means: You're chasing moves instead of waiting for your setup. This causes you to enter lower timeframes, chase momentum, and lose discipline. Miss the move. Protect capital.

🟡 Overconfidence & Ego Risk

"This one looks obvious"
"I can feel the market today"
"I know this will reverse"
"Easy money"
"I've cracked the code"

What it means: You're ego trading and assume you have special market insight. You'll override your plan, increase size, and take stupid risks. This is how accounts blow up.

🟡 Cockiness After Wins

"I made good money, I can risk more"
"I can increase size now"

What it means: One win doesn't mean you've changed. You can go from hero to zero in one trade. Risk stays the same. Always.

🟡 Impatience & Urgency

"Just one more trade"
"I'll stop after this"
"Rushed"

What it means: You're pushing for action instead of waiting. This leads to forced trades, broken plans, and bad entries. Patience is your edge.

🟡 Emotional Carry-Over

"I'm frustrated from yesterday's loss"
"I'm angry at the market"

What it means: Yesterday's emotion is bleeding into today. You're not in a clear headspace. Reduce size. Slow down. Reset yourself.

🟡 Reduced Cognition

"I'm tired"
"I'm exhausted"
"I'm anxious"

What it means: Your mental capacity is reduced. Trading tired is a losing position. Consider sitting out or simulation mode.

Reinforcing Your Trader Identity

MindTrade teaches you to separate behaviour from identity. When you break a rule, it doesn't mean you're a bad trader. It means you had an emotional behaviour that you can fix.

Say this to yourself:

✅ "I am a process-driven trader."
✅ "I do not trade for validation."
✅ "I wait for my setup."
✅ "I protect my capital and emotional state."
✅ "If I follow the plan, I win the day."
✅ "Missing moves is not a loss. Breaking plan is."
